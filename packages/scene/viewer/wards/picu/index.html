    Hopkins lists PICU at Bloomberg 4 South and NICU at Bloomberg 8 North: the same building, four floors apart. No route distance or direct adjacency is claimed.

    Public interior cues

    Hopkins describes private pediatric rooms, family sleeping space and distributed workstations. Its general pediatric-room photo shows blue finishes and seating. Those cues inform this original scene; the photo is not identified as PICU.

    Inferred geometry

    The eight-room arrangement, 26 × 18 m footprint, doors, service panels and all furniture positions are authored for this demo. No measured floor plan, current room mapping or staff sign-off is available. Published PICU bed counts disagree.
